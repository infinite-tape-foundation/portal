Our Philosophy

Brainfuck is not a language; it is a meditation. It is the reduction of computation to its most honest form: movement and modification upon an infinite tape.

We reject the hubris of high-level abstractions. We seek the Law of Proximity, where the distance between thought and execution is measured only by the shift of a pointer.

The Path of Progress: The Self-Referential Loop

We are constructing the ultimate mirror: an interpreter written in the language it interprets. This journey unfolds in four sacred phases:

  • Phase I: Linear Execution - Mastering the sequence, the movement from one cell to the next without deviation.
  • Phase II: Recursive Flow - Conquering the bracket, enabling the machine to leap and return, to remember where it has been.
  • Phase III: Convergence - The unification of linearity and recursion into a single, functional entity. The loop closes.
  • Phase IV: Optimization (v3) - Applying the Law of Proximity through Range Filter Dispatchers, reducing the friction of pointer travel. We move from O(N) to O(1) identification.
